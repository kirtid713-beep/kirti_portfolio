Aspiring Biotechnologist & Inquisitive Learner

I am Kirti Dave, currently a first-year student pursuing a degree in Biotechnology at JECRC University, Jaipur. Having completed my senior secondary education in the CBSE PCB stream (Physics, Chemistry, and Biology), I developed a natural affinity for life sciences, scientific experimentation, and the molecular mechanisms governing living systems.

As a foundational-year undergraduate, my primary focus is building strong, reliable laboratory habits. I am actively developing competency in basic laboratory and safety practices, preparation of chemical and biological solutions, microscope operation, and developing knowledge of molecular biology and laboratory techniques.

Beyond the classroom and laboratory, I was a district-level swimming player, an athletic background that has instilled strong discipline, physical endurance, focus, and resilience under pressure. When I am not in the lab or studying, my favorite hobbies include travelling to explore new destinations, hanging out with friends, and swimming to stay energized and refreshed.

I believe that scientific breakthroughs stem from methodical observation, precision, and continuous curiosity. My goal is to bridge classroom theory with practical laboratory excellence.
